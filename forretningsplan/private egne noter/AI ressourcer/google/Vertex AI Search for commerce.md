Key features

Key features
Conversational Commerce agent
Conversational Commerce agent uses Google's search expertise and Gemini to deliver personalized guidance throughout the shopping journey, maximizing conversions, and revenue. It understands user intent and preferences, provides relevant results, and facilitates product discovery through natural conversation.

With its advanced intent classification, Conversational Commerce agent intelligently switches between traditional product search and conversational interactions, delivering intuitive results optimized for user behavior, your product catalog, and key performance indicators.

UI of user asking for help finding headphones for a marathon with results being different brand and colors and styles of headphones in white, blue, black, and orange
Personalized search and recommendations
Deliver personalized search results and product recommendations that drive your business goals with state-of-the-art natural language processing, Google's unique web signals, Knowledge Graphs, and our commerce-tuned LLMs. Increase revenue with these advanced models, which focus early search results on higher-converting products.

Provide recommendations that adapt to individual shopping behavior, preferences, and historical data to optimize for business objectives, such as maximizing conversion rates, click-through rates, or revenue per session. Recommendations can extend beyond your website to mobile apps, personalized email campaigns, in-store kiosks, and call center applications.

UI of shopping bag with Vertex ai search for commerce enabled recommended results for upsell including a pink blazer, an orange skirt, an orange dress and a hot pink pantsuit set
Merchandising console
Configure search and browse results and recommendations to launch promotions, tune product rankings, and reroute results when items are out of stock. The Merchandising console empowers your business users to react quickly to market trends and customer behavior and lets you instantly deploy and test new strategies to drive business goals.

Catalog improvements
Boost your retail catalog's discoverability and surface more relevant results. We natively enrich your catalog by combining Google Shopping's powerful web crawlers with Google's deep understanding of web content and our unique Vision AI models. This provides essential attributes and taxonomy for improved retrieval, ensuring your catalog connects directly with user needs.

Safety and responsibility, built-in
Security and privacy protocols that ensure your data is isolated with strong access controls; your data is yours alone. We support compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). See terms and the GDPR Resource Center for more information. Also, see our product Service Level Agreement here.
